Several critical vulnerabilities have been identified in popular WordPress plugins and themes, including WPMU DEV Dashboard, Avada, TranslatePress, Pods, and GiveWP. These flaws, such as CVE-2026-76581, could lead to authentication bypass, account takeover, and arbitrary code execution. Website administrators are urged to update their installations immediately to mitigate the risk of compromise.
