The Australian Federal Police (AFP) has charged two men, Louis Michael Gaebler and Ruben Ian Thomson, in connection with the TeamPCP cybercrime group. This group is allegedly responsible for major supply chain attacks in March 2026, compromising open-source security scanners like Trivy and Checkmarx KICS, and the AI gateway LiteLLM. The arrests mark a significant step in disrupting a prolific cybercrime syndicate.
