Cybersecurity researchers have uncovered 19 malicious browser extensions (18 for Chrome, 1 for Edge) designed to steal wallet secrets and drain cryptocurrency. These extensions, active for at least six months, share similar code and tradecraft. Users are advised to review their installed extensions and remove any suspicious ones to protect their digital assets.
