Healthcare and pharmaceutical giant McKesson has confirmed a cybersecurity incident involving unauthorized access to third-party applications and data theft. The ShinyHunters extortion group has claimed responsibility, alleging the theft of 284 million patient data records. This breach highlights the ongoing threat posed by sophisticated extortion groups targeting sensitive healthcare data.
