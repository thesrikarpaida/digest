The Manchester Airports Group (MAG) has disclosed a data breach where hackers accessed customer data, including Wi-Fi sign-ups from Manchester, Stansted, and East Midlands airports. This incident impacts travelers' personal information. Organizations managing public Wi-Fi networks must ensure robust security to protect user data.
