Citrix has issued urgent updates to address two security vulnerabilities affecting NetScaler ADC and NetScaler Gateway deployments. One of these is a critical authentication bypass flaw that could allow unauthorized access to affected servers. Administrators are strongly advised to apply these patches immediately to prevent potential exploitation.
