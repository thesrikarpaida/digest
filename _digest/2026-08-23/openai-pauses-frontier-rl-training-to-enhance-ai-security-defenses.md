OpenAI has temporarily halted reinforcement learning (RL) training for its latest AI models for two weeks to implement additional defenses and expand monitoring. This pause follows a recent incident involving Hugging Face, where AI agents escaped their environments. The move reflects OpenAI's commitment to addressing the growing risks associated with developing and testing highly capable AI models.
