Apple has issued threat notifications to customers in 110 countries, alerting them to suspected spyware attacks targeting their devices. This marks an unprecedented scale for such warnings, indicating a widespread and significant threat. The alerts underscore the persistent danger of state-sponsored or sophisticated commercial spyware operations.
