A campaign dubbed 'Offside Wallet Theft Factory' has been discovered, involving 40 malicious Mozilla Firefox extensions masquerading as legitimate Web3 products like OKX and Rabby Wallet. These extensions are designed to steal cryptocurrency wallet secrets. The Socket Threat Research team found that these add-ons share source code and infrastructure, indicating a coordinated effort to target crypto users.
