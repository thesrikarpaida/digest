Ubiquiti has released updates to address multiple critical security vulnerabilities across its UniFi product line, including Connect, Talk, Access, Protect, and OS. One flaw, CVE-2026-50746, has a CVSS score of 10.0 and is an improper access control vulnerability in UniFi Connect. These issues could lead to privilege escalation and arbitrary command execution, necessitating immediate patching for all affected systems.
