Coal India's 'intelligent CCTV platform,' developed by DeepSight AI Labs and Accenture, was found to use plaintext passwords and lacked API authentication. This severe security oversight exposed the entire camera network, potentially allowing unauthorized access and control. The incident highlights fundamental security failures in critical infrastructure deployments, especially those integrating AI technologies.
