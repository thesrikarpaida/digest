Microsoft has analyzed 'GigaWiper,' a destructive Windows backdoor that combines three older malicious programs into one. This sophisticated malware offers operators a choice of commands: full disk wiping, overwriting the Windows drive, or deploying fake ransomware that scrambles files without saving the key. GigaWiper's modular design allows for varied and devastating attacks, making it a significant threat.
