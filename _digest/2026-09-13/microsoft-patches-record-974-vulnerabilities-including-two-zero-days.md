Microsoft's September 2026 Patch Tuesday addressed an unprecedented 974 vulnerabilities across its product portfolio, including two actively exploited zero-days. This massive update includes 113 critical-severity flaws, with a significant number affecting Windows, Office, SQL, and Developer Tools. The sheer volume of fixes raises concerns about organizations' ability to prioritize and deploy patches effectively, especially as AI accelerates vulnerability discovery.
