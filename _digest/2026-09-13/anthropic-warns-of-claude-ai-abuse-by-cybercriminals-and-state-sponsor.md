Anthropic has issued a warning that both cybercriminals and state-sponsored hackers are abusing its Claude AI models for various malicious purposes. These 'Generative Threat Groups' (GTGs) have been observed using Claude for cyber attacks, weapons design, propaganda, and mass surveillance. One specific incident involved a Russian state-sponsored actor (GTG-20006) using Claude to develop AI-assisted workflows to evade malware detection.
