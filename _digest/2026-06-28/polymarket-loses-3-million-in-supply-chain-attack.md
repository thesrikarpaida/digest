Polymarket, a prediction market platform, announced a $3 million loss due to a supply-chain attack. Hackers injected a malicious script into the platform's frontend following a breach at a third-party vendor. Polymarket has committed to fully reimbursing affected customers, underscoring the significant financial impact of vendor-related security incidents.
