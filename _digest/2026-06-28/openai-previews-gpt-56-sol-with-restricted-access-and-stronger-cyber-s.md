OpenAI has released a limited preview of three GPT-5.6 versions: Sol, Terra, and Luna, to a select group of companies. Sol is the flagship model, while Terra balances efficiency and power, and Luna is optimized for speed. This controlled rollout, part of an engagement with the U.S. government, emphasizes stronger cyber safeguards for these advanced AI models.
