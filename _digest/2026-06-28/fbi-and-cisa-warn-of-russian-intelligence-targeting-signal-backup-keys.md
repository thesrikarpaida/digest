The FBI and CISA have issued an updated warning regarding a Russian intelligence phishing campaign. This campaign now aims to steal Signal Backup Recovery Keys, allowing attackers to access victims' historical messages. Once compromised, the key grants persistent access to an account's message history and enables account takeover. This represents an escalation in the threat actor's capabilities.
