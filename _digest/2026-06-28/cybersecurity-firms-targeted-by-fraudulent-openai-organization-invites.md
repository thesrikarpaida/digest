Threat actors are creating fake OpenAI tenants that impersonate legitimate companies. They then invite employees to join these fraudulent organizations, likely as a ploy to trick targets into submitting sensitive company information in chats and projects. This social engineering tactic leverages the trust associated with AI platforms to facilitate data theft.
