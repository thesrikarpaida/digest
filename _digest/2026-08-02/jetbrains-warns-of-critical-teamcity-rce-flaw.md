JetBrains has issued a warning about a critical authentication bypass vulnerability in TeamCity On-Premises. This flaw could be exploited by attackers to achieve remote code execution (RCE). Organizations using TeamCity On-Premises are urged to apply the necessary security updates immediately to prevent potential compromise of their continuous integration/continuous delivery (CI/CD) pipelines.
