A Chinese-speaking threat actor is leveraging the DeepSeek AI model and the open-source Hermes Agent framework to conduct autonomous cyberattacks on exposed servers. After initial Telegram instructions, the AI agent autonomously identifies internet-facing systems and selects public exploits, demonstrating a significant advancement in automated offensive capabilities. This marks a concerning trend where AI models are being weaponized for cyber warfare with minimal human intervention.
