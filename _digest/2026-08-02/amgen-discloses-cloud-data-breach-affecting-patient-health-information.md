Pharmaceutical company Amgen has disclosed a data breach resulting from threat actors stealing corporate and patient health information. The stolen data was stored in multiple cloud systems operated by third-party service providers. This incident highlights the ongoing challenges of securing sensitive data in cloud environments, especially when relying on third-party vendors, and the critical need for robust supply chain security measures in healthcare.
