The image-sharing platform Gyazo, operated by Helpfeel, confirmed a data breach that exposed approximately 23.62 million user records, including email addresses and password hashes. Additionally, about 490 million image metadata records, primarily from images uploaded before January 2019, were compromised. The breach was caused by an exploited server vulnerability, leading to a significant exposure of sensitive user data.
