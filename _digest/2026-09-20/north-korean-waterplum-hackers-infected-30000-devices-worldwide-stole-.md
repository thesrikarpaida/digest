A joint law enforcement advisory reveals that the North Korean hacking group WaterPlum compromised at least 30,000 devices globally between December 2025 and July 2026. The group exfiltrated over $10.7 million in stolen cryptocurrency, transferring the funds to North Korea. This activity highlights the persistent and financially motivated cyber operations conducted by state-sponsored actors.
