This blog post explores how modern tools and techniques are making software reverse engineering more accessible to a broader audience. It discusses the impact of advanced disassemblers, debuggers, and automated analysis frameworks on the field. The author argues that this 'democratization' lowers the barrier to entry for aspiring security researchers and offensive practitioners. While beneficial for learning and defense, it also implies that more actors, including malicious ones, can engage in sophisticated reverse engineering.
