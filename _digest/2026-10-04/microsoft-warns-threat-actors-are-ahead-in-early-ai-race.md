Microsoft reports that cyberattackers are currently leveraging artificial intelligence more effectively than defenders. AI is enabling threat actors to accelerate vulnerability discovery, malware development, and post-compromise activities. This gives attackers a significant advantage, as security teams struggle to keep pace with the rapid evolution of AI-powered offensive capabilities. The warning emphasizes the urgent need for security professionals to integrate AI into their defensive strategies to counter this growing imbalance.
