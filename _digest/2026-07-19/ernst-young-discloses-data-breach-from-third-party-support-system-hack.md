Ernst & Young (EY) is notifying customers about a data breach resulting from the compromise of a third-party support ticket system used by its IT personnel. This incident highlights the pervasive risk of supply chain attacks and the importance of securing third-party vendors. The breach could expose sensitive customer information handled through the support system.
