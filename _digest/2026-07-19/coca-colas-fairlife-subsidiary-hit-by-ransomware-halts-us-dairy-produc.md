The Coca-Cola Company has disclosed that its Fairlife dairy subsidiary was impacted by a ransomware attack, leading to a temporary suspension of production across the United States. This incident underscores the significant operational and supply chain disruptions that ransomware attacks can cause, even to major global corporations. The attack highlights the ongoing threat to critical infrastructure and manufacturing sectors.
