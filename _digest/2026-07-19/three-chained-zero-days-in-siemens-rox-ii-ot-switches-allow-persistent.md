Palo Alto Networks' Unit 42 has detailed a trilogy of chained zero-day vulnerabilities in Siemens ROX II OT switches. These flaws allow for privilege escalation and persistent root access to the industrial control systems. The discovery underscores the critical need for robust security in operational technology (OT) environments, where vulnerabilities can have severe real-world impacts.
