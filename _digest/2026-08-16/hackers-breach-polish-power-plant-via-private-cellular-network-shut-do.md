Attackers successfully breached a Polish combined heat and power plant by exploiting its private cellular network, leading to the shutdown of a steam turbine and process-water treatment system. The incident highlights the vulnerability of critical infrastructure connected via private cellular networks. Despite the intrusion, the plant managed to restore operations without impacting heat supply to residents.
