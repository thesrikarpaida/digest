The DeadLock ransomware group is leveraging decentralized infrastructure, specifically Polygon smart contracts, to manage victim communications and data leak operations. This approach, which combines the Session messaging network with blockchain-backed services, aims to enhance the operational resilience of their extortion process. This marks a novel use of blockchain by ransomware groups to evade disruption.
