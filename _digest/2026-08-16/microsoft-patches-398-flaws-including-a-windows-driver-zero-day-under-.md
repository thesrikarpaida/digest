Microsoft's August 2026 Patch Tuesday addressed 398 vulnerabilities, including one actively exploited Windows kernel driver zero-day (CVE-2026-68820) that allows for privilege escalation to SYSTEM. This critical patch highlights the ongoing threat of in-the-wild exploits. Additionally, two other publicly known vulnerabilities were patched, emphasizing the importance of timely updates.
