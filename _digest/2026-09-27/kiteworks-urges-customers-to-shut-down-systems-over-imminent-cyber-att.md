Kiteworks (formerly Accellion) has issued an urgent advisory for customers to temporarily shut down their systems for several hours due to credible threat intelligence about an imminent cyber attack. The warning, received from federal intelligence authorities, suggests a potential zero-day attack targeting Kiteworks systems. This proactive measure aims to mitigate the risk of compromise, underscoring the severity of the intelligence received.
