Major AI developers Google, Anthropic, and OpenAI have announced new cybersecurity-focused AI models and initiatives. Google introduced Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber, available to trusted defenders via the Fairwind Program. These efforts aim to provide high-priority defenders with early access to advanced AI models to enhance cybersecurity, signaling a collaborative approach to leveraging AI for defense.
