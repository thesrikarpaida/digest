Thomson Reuters disclosed a breach of its C-Track court case management platform, affecting courts in 11 U.S. states, the U.S. Virgin Islands, and Ontario, Canada. An unauthorized party obtained files in March 2026, with discovery in June. A subset of court records could contain individuals' names, Social Security Numbers, and potentially sealed data, raising serious privacy concerns for those involved in legal proceedings.
