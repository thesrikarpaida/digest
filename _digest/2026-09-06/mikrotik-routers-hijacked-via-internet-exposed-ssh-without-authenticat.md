Attackers are exploiting MikroTik routers with internet-exposed SSH services to gain full administrative control without requiring authentication. CERT Polska issued a warning about these attacks, which have been observed since at least September 2nd. This vulnerability allows for complete compromise of affected devices, posing a significant risk to network infrastructure.
