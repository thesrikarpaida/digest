The U.S. Department of Justice has seized the domains CFAKE.com and SOCFAKE.com, which allegedly hosted nonconsensual AI-generated nude images and videos. This action marks a significant enforcement under the TAKE IT DOWN Act, targeting platforms facilitating the creation and distribution of illicit deepfake content. The seizure underscores growing legal efforts to combat the misuse of AI for harmful purposes.
