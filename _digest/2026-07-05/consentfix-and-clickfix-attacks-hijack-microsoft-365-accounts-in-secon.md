ConsentFix and ClickFix attacks are rapidly compromising Microsoft 365 accounts by stealing tokens through fake prompts and OAuth flows. These techniques effectively bypass multi-factor authentication (MFA) by tricking users into granting malicious applications access or executing commands. The attacks highlight the need for enhanced user awareness and robust detection mechanisms against sophisticated social engineering and token theft.
