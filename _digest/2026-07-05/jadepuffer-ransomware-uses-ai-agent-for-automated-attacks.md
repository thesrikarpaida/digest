Researchers have identified JadePuffer as the first documented ransomware operation conducted entirely by a large language model (LLM) agent. This AI agent automates the entire attack chain, from initial compromise and credential theft to lateral movement and database encryption. This development signifies a new frontier in ransomware, where AI can independently execute complex malicious operations, posing a significant challenge to traditional defense mechanisms.
