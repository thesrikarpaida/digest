Microsoft is accelerating its quantum-safe security roadmap, aiming to replace existing encryption standards by 2029 due to rapid advancements in quantum computing. This move, announced by Microsoft Azure CTO Mark Russinovich, underscores the growing urgency to prepare for a 'quantum apocalypse' where current cryptographic methods could be broken. The shift will impact a wide range of services and products, requiring significant industry-wide migration efforts.
