Adobe has released a significant security bulletin, APSB26-68, for ColdFusion, addressing a multitude of vulnerabilities, some of which are critical. The sheer volume of fixes, and some unmentioned issues, indicates a complex security landscape for ColdFusion users. Organizations are strongly advised to prioritize these updates to mitigate risks of arbitrary code execution and other severe impacts.
