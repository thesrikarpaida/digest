A former Member of the European Parliament, Stelios Kouloglou, was repeatedly targeted and hacked with Pegasus spyware while serving on a committee investigating commercial surveillance tool abuse. Forensic analysis confirmed the compromise, highlighting the persistent threat of state-sponsored spyware even against individuals actively working on related security issues. This incident underscores the sophisticated nature of such attacks and their potential to undermine democratic processes.
