Levi Strauss & Co. (Levi's) has confirmed a cyberattack where hackers used social engineering to compromise three employee accounts. The attackers gained access to and stole corporate data stored on these employees' machines. This incident highlights the persistent threat of social engineering tactics against even large corporations.
