The North Carolina Ports Authority has confirmed a cyberattack that disrupted its IT systems and slowed operations at Port of Wilmington, Port of Morehead City, and Charlotte Inland Port. The incident highlights the vulnerability of critical infrastructure to cyber threats. Details on the nature of the attack and its full impact are still emerging.
