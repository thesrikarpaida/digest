The North Korean state-sponsored hacking group ScarCruft (APT37) is employing spear-phishing tactics impersonating Microsoft Account security notifications. These deceptive emails are designed to deliver a new malware variant named NarwhalRAT. The attack aims to create urgency and concern, tricking recipients into opening malicious attachments or clicking links that lead to the deployment of the RAT, enabling remote control and data exfiltration.
