Apple has released an update for its Beats Studio Buds to address CVE-2025-20701 (CVSS score: 8.8), a high-severity vulnerability in the Airoha Bluetooth audio SDK. This flaw could allow nearby attackers to pair a Bluetooth audio device without user consent and potentially eavesdrop via the microphone. Users are advised to update their Beats Studio Buds firmware immediately.
