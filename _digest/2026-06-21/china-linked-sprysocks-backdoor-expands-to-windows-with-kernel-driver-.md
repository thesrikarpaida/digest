The China-nexus threat group 'FishMonger' has deployed previously undocumented Windows variants of the SprySOCKS backdoor, which was thought to be Linux-only. These new variants, internally named WIN_DRV and WIN_PLUS, utilize kernel drivers to evade detection and establish stealthy communication over TCP and UDP. The expansion of SprySOCKS to Windows, coupled with its advanced evasion techniques, poses a significant threat to government targets in various regions.
