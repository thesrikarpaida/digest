An update on the 'FortiBleed' incident reveals that 75,000 Fortinet firewalls had admin passwords cracked and exposed. Contrary to initial statements from Fortinet suggesting the data was from prior breaches and brute-forcing, new evidence indicates a more direct compromise. Logs from impacted organizations suggest a more sophisticated attack, with CloudSEK providing further insights into the threat actor infrastructure. CISA has also issued warnings, urging Fortinet customers to secure their devices.
