Market intelligence platform Klue has confirmed a security incident where threat actors stole OAuth tokens used to connect to customers' Salesforce environments. The new 'Icarus' extortion group has claimed responsibility for the attack, which has resulted in the theft of Salesforce CRM data from multiple organizations, including cybersecurity vendor Huntress. Salesforce has disabled the Klue Battlecards app integration as a protective measure, urging customers to revoke any connected Klue OAuth tokens.
