Meta's WhatsApp is pursuing legal action against NSO Group, accusing the company of renewed targeting of users with its Pegasus surveillance spyware. WhatsApp plans to ask a US court to hold NSO Group in contempt, alleging that the company used WhatsApp to lure targets into downloading the spyware. This development reignites concerns about the proliferation and misuse of sophisticated surveillance tools by state-backed entities.
