Microsoft has linked the recent Mastra AI supply chain attack, which compromised over 140 npm packages, to the North Korean hacking group Sapphire Sleet, also known as BlueNoroff. This attribution highlights the increasing focus of state-sponsored actors on supply chain attacks targeting AI development ecosystems. The compromise of numerous npm packages could have far-reaching implications for projects relying on the Mastra framework.
