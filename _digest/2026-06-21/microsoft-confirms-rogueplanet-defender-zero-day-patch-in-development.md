Microsoft has officially acknowledged a zero-day privilege escalation vulnerability in its Malware Protection Engine, codenamed RoguePlanet and assigned CVE-2026-50656 (CVSS score: 7.8). The company is actively working on a patch to address this flaw in Microsoft Defender. Users should monitor for the upcoming security update to mitigate this risk.
