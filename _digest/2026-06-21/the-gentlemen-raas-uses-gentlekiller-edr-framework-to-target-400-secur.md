The Gentlemen ransomware-as-a-service (RaaS) operation is actively developing and deploying a sophisticated suite of EDR killers, centered around the 'GentleKiller' framework. This framework targets and disables over 400 security processes, allowing affiliates to impair system defenses before deploying ransomware. The use of such a mature and extensive EDR evasion toolkit highlights the increasing sophistication of ransomware operations and their focus on bypassing endpoint security solutions.
