The Texas Parks and Wildlife Department (TPWD) has disclosed a data breach affecting its license system vendor, exposing personal information for over three million individuals. The compromised data includes driver's license details, posing a significant risk for identity theft and other malicious activities. This incident underscores the critical importance of third-party vendor security and robust data protection measures for government agencies.
