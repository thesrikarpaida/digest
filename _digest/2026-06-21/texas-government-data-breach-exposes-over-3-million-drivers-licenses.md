The Texas Parks and Wildlife Department (TPWD) has disclosed a data breach at its license system vendor, impacting over three million individuals. The breach exposed personal information, including driver's license data. This incident highlights the risks associated with third-party vendors and the critical need for robust security measures in government systems.
