Praetorian has open-sourced Sulla, an SMB secret scanner designed to discover credentials exposed in SMB shares across enterprise networks. Built on their Titus Go library, Sulla offers an easy-to-use, adaptable, and high-performance standalone binary. This tool addresses the common challenge faced by network penetration testers in efficiently reviewing network shares for sensitive material, making it a valuable addition to offensive security arsenals.
