Since late 2025, malicious wallpapers have been rapidly spreading through the Steam Workshop, compromising gamers' accounts. Attackers are primarily targeting users in China and Russia by embedding malware within custom content shared on the platform. This campaign highlights a novel vector for malware distribution within gaming communities, leveraging trusted platforms to deliver threats. Gamers should exercise caution when downloading user-generated content and ensure their security software is up to date.
