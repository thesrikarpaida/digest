The Gentlemen ransomware-as-a-service (RaaS) operation is employing a sophisticated suite of EDR killers, collectively known as 'GentleKiller', to neutralize endpoint detection and response solutions. This framework, distributed to affiliates, targets over 400 security processes, allowing the ransomware to deploy effectively. The use of such advanced evasion tools highlights the evolving threat landscape in ransomware attacks.
