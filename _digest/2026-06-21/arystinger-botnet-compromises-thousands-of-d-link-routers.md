A new botnet, dubbed AryStinger, has been discovered infecting over 4,000 outdated D-Link routers globally. This malware transforms compromised devices into proxy servers for malicious traffic, highlighting the ongoing risk posed by unpatched and end-of-life network hardware. The botnet's emergence underscores the critical need for users to update their router firmware and replace unsupported devices to prevent them from being co-opted into malicious infrastructure.
