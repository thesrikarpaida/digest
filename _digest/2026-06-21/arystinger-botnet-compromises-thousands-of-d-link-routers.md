A newly discovered botnet, 'AryStinger', has infected over 4,000 outdated D-Link routers worldwide. These compromised devices are being used as proxies for malicious traffic, indicating a widespread abuse of vulnerable network infrastructure. The botnet's activity underscores the importance of regularly updating router firmware and replacing end-of-life devices.
