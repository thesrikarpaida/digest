A new ransomware variant, 'Prinz Eugen,' has emerged with a unique encryption strategy. This ransomware prioritizes recently modified files for encryption, potentially maximizing immediate impact on active users. Notably, it does not leave a ransom note on the compromised system, making incident response and victim communication more challenging. This tactic suggests an attempt to quickly disrupt operations and obscure the attacker's identity.
