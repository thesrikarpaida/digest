International law enforcement, led by Dutch authorities, has successfully disrupted the malicious infrastructure of the SocGholish malware operation. This extensive effort, part of Operation Endgame, resulted in the cleanup of nearly 15,000 infected WordPress websites and the disruption of over 100 servers and domains used for malware distribution. The operation also provided Have I Been Pwned with 154,000 impacted email addresses and over half a million previously unseen passwords.
