JCPenney and associated brands were targeted in a ShinyHunters 'pay or leak' extortion campaign in June 2026. Data allegedly obtained through a critical zero-day vulnerability in Oracle PeopleSoft was publicly released. The breach exposed 368,418 corporate and personal email addresses, names, dates of birth, Social Security numbers, phone numbers, and home addresses, primarily from internal HR systems impacting current and former employees.
