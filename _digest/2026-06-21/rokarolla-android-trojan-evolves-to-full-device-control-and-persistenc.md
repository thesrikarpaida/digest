The Rokarolla Android Trojan has significantly evolved, now capable of achieving full device control and persistence. Initially spread via fake TikTok and Chrome downloads, this malware combines banking fraud capabilities with extensive device surveillance and remote control features. Its enhanced functionality poses a severe threat to Android users, allowing attackers to steal sensitive information and manipulate devices remotely.
