Apple has released a security update for its Beats Studio Buds to patch CVE-2025-20701, a high-severity vulnerability (CVSS 8.8). This flaw, an incorrect authorization issue in the Airoha Bluetooth audio SDK, could allow nearby attackers to pair a Bluetooth audio device without user consent and eavesdrop via the microphone. Users are advised to update their Beats Studio Buds firmware immediately.
