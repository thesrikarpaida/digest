This site is part personal journal, part **weekly intelligence feed** at the
intersection of offensive security and AI.

There's a few kinds of content here:

- The **DIGEST** home page - the full feed of short, curated news items, each
  with a severity rating and links to the original sources.
- **TOP PICKS** - the highest-impact items, surfaced on their own page.
- **DEEP DIVES** - occasional long-form analysis: kill chains, malware
  breakdowns, and post-mortems on the bigger stories.
- **WRITEUPS** - my own write-ups (TryHackMe rooms, notes, and the occasional
  longer piece), separate from the auto-curated digest.

### How the digest works

The digest items are generated by an automated pipeline that pulls from a large
set of offensive-security and security-engineering RSS feeds (based on
[Graham Helton's feed list](https://grahamhelton.com/blog/rss-feeds)),
deduplicates them, and uses a language model to rewrite headlines, write short
summaries, and assign a severity and tags. It runs weekly, so the feed stays
current on its own.
